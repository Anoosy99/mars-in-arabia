                The peninsula scan

                Each 0.1° cell gets an aridity factor from NASA POWER rainfall, a bare-ground factor from NASA MODIS vegetation index (NDVI, the greenest of four 2025 seasons, decoded to real values), and a land mask. Three archetypes are scored: volcanic (dark basalt surfaces with moderate slope), smooth plains (very gentle, bright, arid ground, like landing sites) and rugged (steep slopes with high local relief). Each archetype is calibrated to its own 99.9th percentile, and the six field sites are used as a check.

                What no place on Earth can fake.

                0.38 g

                Gravity on Mars. The Moon is 0.17 g. Every site here pulls 2.6 to 6 times harder.

                0.6%

                Mars surface pressure compared with Earth. The Moon has none at all.

                No shield

                Neither world has Earth's magnetic field or thick air, so surface radiation is far higher.

                How sure we are

                • Facts are sourced. Site facts, NASA base regions, map units and climate values come from the sources listed under the method.
                • Pairings are our proposal. Which Saudi site trains crews for which Moon or Mars base site is the team's recommendation, built on those facts. NASA has not endorsed it.
                • Terrain is compared at 460–570 m pixels on Mars (MOLA's limit). Small features, such as the Wabar craters or the inside of a lava tube, are below that size and are not in the terrain score.
                • The finder check is partly circular. The volcanic archetype looks for dark basalt, so known lava fields score high by design. The check shows it works; it does not prove it is unbiased.
                • Umm Jirsan's position is approximate, and NASA POWER climate is coarse, about 50 km per cell.

                An analog does not have to match everything. It has to be honest about what it matches.
